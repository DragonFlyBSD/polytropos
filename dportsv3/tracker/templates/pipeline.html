These are inventories, not stages of a queue. Work does not advance through them in order: triage and patch are separate jobs (a triage that decides to patch enqueues a new row and finishes), a failed verification or fresh operator context enqueues new work on a problem that had already been through once, and an issue can reopen after resolving when its fingerprint comes back. A single port can be counted in several of these at once, and the totals do not add up to anything.
